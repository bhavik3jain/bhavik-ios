# Multitrack Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Multitrack keeps your trips, guides, workouts, shows, orders, fuel logs, points and finances. This policy explains where that information goes. In short: it stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account, and the developer never receives any of it.

## What the developer collects

Nothing. Multitrack has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code that does. There is no Multitrack server: the developer can't see anything you put in the app.

## Where your information is kept

- **On your devices**, in the app's own storage.
- **In your iCloud account**, so it syncs between your devices and survives deleting and reinstalling the app. Multitrack uses your private iCloud database, which only you can read. Apple's [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) covers iCloud.
- **Some settings**, such as which trackers show and the Apple Intelligence switches, sync through iCloud too.
- **API keys you enter** are kept in your iCloud Keychain. See "Your API keys" below.

## Sharing with people you choose

You can share a trip, a guide, a vehicle, a watch list, or a Points or Finance household with someone through iCloud. Everyone on a share can see and change everything in it: for a household, that's every person, account and balance in it. Your name as it appears in iCloud is shown to them. You can stop sharing or remove someone at any time, and someone you've shared with can leave.

When something shared changes, Multitrack can notify the others on it. Some of these notices are sent by iCloud itself and include the shared item's name, for example "Rome & Amalfi was updated".

## Services the app contacts

Multitrack contacts other services only to do something you've asked for. This is what each one receives:

- **Apple iCloud**: your information and settings, as described above.
- **Apple Weather**: the location of a trip's or guide's places, to show their forecast.
- **Apple Maps**: what you type into a place search and the area to search in. When you ask for places near you, that area can be based on your location.
- **The Movie Database (TMDB)**: the titles you search for, and TMDB's ID numbers for the shows and films you track, to fetch episode lists, details and artwork. These requests use your own TMDB API key.
- **FedEx**: the tracking numbers of your FedEx orders, to fetch their status. These requests use your own FedEx API key.
- **Yahoo Finance**: a request for the latest gold and silver futures prices. Nothing about you is in it.
- **Carrier websites**: when you open an order's tracking page, it loads from UPS, USPS or FedEx in an in-app browser, like any web page.

Like any internet request, each of these reaches the service from your device's IP address. Each service's own privacy policy covers what it does with a request.

## Your API keys

TV and Orders can use API keys of your own: a TMDB key for show and film details, and a FedEx key and secret for tracking FedEx orders. Both are optional.

- You get them yourself, from free TMDB and FedEx developer accounts, and enter them in TV Settings or Orders Settings.
- They're stored in your iCloud Keychain, which Apple encrypts end to end, so they reach your other devices and survive reinstalling the app.
- They're sent only to the service they belong to, with each request to it. Never to the developer, and never anywhere else.
- You can remove them from TV Settings or Orders Settings at any time.

## Location

Multitrack asks for your location only when you open a guide's map or a trip's Nearby list, and only while you're using the app. It's used on your device to show where you are and how far away places are. It isn't stored, and it's sent nowhere except as the search area described under Apple Maps.

## Apple Intelligence

On devices with Apple Intelligence, Trips and Finance can write reviews and suggestions with Apple's on-device model. That happens entirely on your device: nothing you've entered is sent to Apple or anyone else for it. Finance's written reviews are saved on the device only, never in iCloud. You can turn this off in Settings, under Apple Intelligence.

## Notifications

Finance's monthly reminder and TV's new-episode alerts are scheduled on your device. Notices about shared changes are described above. You choose whether to allow notifications, and can change that at any time in your device's settings.

## Files you import and export

Files you import, such as a Fuelly export or a TV watch history, are read on your device. Reports, PDFs and spreadsheets the app makes are created on your device and go only where you send or save them. On a Mac, Finance's Export to Numbers fills a spreadsheet in the Numbers app on that Mac.

## Keeping and deleting your information

Your information is kept until you delete it. Delete something in the app and it's removed from your devices and from iCloud. To remove everything:

- Delete the app from each of your devices to remove what's stored on them.
- Delete Multitrack's data from your iCloud storage settings. On iPhone, that's in Settings, under your name, then iCloud, then the storage list.

Things you've shared live in your iCloud account; leaving something shared with you removes your access to it. Because the developer holds none of your information, there's nothing for the developer to delete, but you're welcome to ask about it.

## Children

Multitrack isn't directed at children under 13, and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and wherever it's published, with a new effective date.
